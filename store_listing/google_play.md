## Varsayılan + en-US

### Uygulama adı (30)
Oxyn: Battery & Storage Care

### Kısa açıklama (80)
Battery info, storage cleanup you control, device status, and phone news.

### Uzun açıklama
Oxyn helps you understand battery and storage, free up space safely, and stay current with phone news — without fake speed boosts or invented hardware scores.

BATTERY
• Charge level and, when the device reports it, temperature
• Battery details only from the device — Oxyn does not invent a health percentage
• Optional charge reminder (for example at 80%)
• Shortcut to system battery settings

STORAGE CLEANUP
• Find similar photos, large files, and screenshots
• You choose what to delete — nothing is removed without confirmation
• Clear app cache to free space
• Results depend on gallery access and how your phone stores media

DEVICE STATUS
• A simple status summary from available signals (charge, storage)
• If a value is missing, Oxyn shows that it is unavailable instead of guessing
• Optional AI device tips from those same numbers (where available)

PHONE NEWS & TRIVIA
• Smartphone headlines from public sources
• Short trivia to learn about phones

OXYN PLUS (OPTIONAL)
• More AI analyses and fewer cleanup limits
• Extra in-app customization
• Subscription terms are shown at purchase

IMPORTANT
Oxyn is not antivirus software and does not claim to remove malware.
It does not guarantee faster performance or longer battery life.
Any score is guidance from available device data, not a laboratory measurement.

Privacy: photos and files stay on your device. See https://oxynapp.net/privacy-policy.html
