## App Review Notes (App Store Connect > App Review Information > Notes)
Oxyn 1.6.0 is a subscription-only app. After the short onboarding, a subscription screen is shown and the app's features require an active Oxyn Plus auto-renewable subscription (weekly, monthly or yearly, in the same subscription group).

To review the app, please purchase any plan with your Sandbox Apple Account on the subscription screen. After the purchase the app opens automatically. The same screen contains "Restore Purchases" and links to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use (EULA). No login or account is required.

Ads have been removed in this version. The app does not use App Tracking Transparency.
