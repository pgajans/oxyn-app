Kaskelo

One tap. The whole chain falls.

Kaskelo is a domino chain puzzle. Every board has a solution with a set number of taps: pick the right tile, turn the arrows, avoid the figures and watch the cascade.

  • Thousands of handmade-feeling boards that get smarter as you go
  • A chess map: topple 32 pieces, from pawns to the king
  • Heavy tiles, walls, guards and turnable arrows
  • Free to play; works offline
  • Turkish and English
